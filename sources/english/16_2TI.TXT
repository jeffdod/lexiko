01:01 Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ through the will of God, according to the promise of the life which is in Christ Jesus,
01:02 to Timothy, my beloved child: Grace, mercy, peace, from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.
01:03 I thank God, whom I serve from my forefathers in a pure conscience, how unceasing is my remembrance of thee in my supplications, night and day
01:04 longing to see thee, remembering thy tears, that I may be filled with joy;
01:05 being reminded of the unfeigned faith that is in thee; which dwelt first in thy grandmother Lois, and thy mother Eunice; and, I am persuaded, in thee also.
01:06 For which cause I put thee in remembrance that thou stir up the gift of God, which is in thee through the laying on of my hands.
01:07 For God gave us not a spirit of fearfulness; but of power and love and discipline.
01:08 Be not ashamed therefore of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me his prisoner: but suffer hardship with the gospel according to the power of God;
01:09 who saved us, and called us with a holy calling, not according to our works, but according to his own purpose and grace, which was given us in Christ Jesus before times eternal,
01:10 but hath now been manifested by the appearing of our Savior Jesus Christ, who abolished death, and brought life and immortality to light through the gospel,
01:11 whereunto I was appointed a preacher, and an apostle, and a teacher of the Gentiles.
01:12 For which cause I suffer also these things: yet I am not ashamed; for I know him whom I have believed, and I am persuaded that he is able to guard that which I have committed unto him against that day.
01:13 Hold the pattern of sound words which thou hast heard from me, in faith and love which is in Christ Jesus.
01:14 That good thing which was committed unto [thee] guard through the Holy Spirit which dwelleth in us.
01:15 This thou knowest, that all that are in Asia turned away from me; of whom are Phygelus and Hermogenes.
01:16 The Lord grant mercy unto the house of Onesiphorus: for he oft refreshed me, and was not ashamed of my chain;
01:17 but, when he was in Rome, he sought me very diligently, and found me
01:18 (the Lord grant unto him to find mercy of the Lord in that day); and in how many things he ministered at Ephesus, thou knowest very well.
02:01 Thou therefore, my child, be strengthened in the grace that is in Christ Jesus.
02:02 And the things which thou hast heard from me among many witnesses, the same commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also.
02:03 Thou therefore suffer hardship, as a good soldier of Jesus Christ.
02:04 No soldier on service entangleth himself in the affairs of [this] life; that he may please him who enrolled him as a soldier.
02:05 And if also a man contend in the games, he is not crowned, except he have contended lawfully.
02:06 The husbandman that laboreth must be the first to partake of the fruits.
02:07 Consider what I say; may the Lord indeed give thee understanding in all things.
02:08 Remember Jesus Christ, risen from the dead, of the seed of David, according to my gospel:
02:09 wherein I suffer hardship unto bonds, as a malefactor; but the word of God is not bound.
02:10 Therefore I endure all things for the elect's sake, that they also may obtain the salvation which is in Christ Jesus with eternal glory.
02:11 Faithful is the saying: For if we died with him, we shall also live with him:
02:12 if we endure, we shall also reign with him: if we deny him, he also will deny us:
02:13 if we are faithless, he abideth faithful: he cannot deny himself.
02:14 Of these things put them in remembrance, charging [them] in the sight of the Lord, that they strive not about words, to no profit, to the subverting of them that hear.
02:15 Give diligence to present thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, handling aright the word of truth.
02:16 But shun profane babblings: for they will proceed further in ungodliness,
02:17 and their word will eat as doth a gangrene: of whom is Hymenaeus and Philetus;
02:18 men who concerning the truth have erred, saying that the resurrection is past already, and overthrow the faith of some.
02:19 Howbeit the firm foundation of God standeth, having this seal, The Lord knoweth them that are his: and, Let everyone that nameth the name of the Lord depart from unrighteousness.
02:20 Now in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of earth; and some unto honor, and some unto dishonor.
02:21 If a man therefore purge himself from these, he shall be a vessel unto honor, sanctified, and meet for the master's use, prepared unto every good work.
02:22 But flee youthful lusts, and follow after righteousness, faith, love, peace, with them that call on the Lord out of a pure heart.
02:23 But foolish and ignorant questionings refuse, knowing that they gender strifes.
02:24 And the Lord's servant must not strive, but be gentle toward all, apt to teach, forbearing,
02:25 in meekness correcting them that oppose themselves; if peradventure God may give them repentance unto the knowledge of the truth,
02:26 and they may recover themselves out of the snare of the devil, having been taken captive by him unto his will.
03:01 But know this, that in the last days grievous times shall come.
03:02 For men shall be lovers of self, lovers of money, boastful, haughty, railers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy,
03:03 without natural affection, implacable, slanderers, without self-control, fierce, no lovers of good,
03:04 traitors, headstrong, puffed up, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God;
03:05 holding a form of godliness, but having denied the power thereof: from these also turn away.
03:06 For of these are they that creep into houses, and take captive silly women laden with sins, led away by diverse lusts,
03:07 ever learning, and never able to come to the knowledge of the truth.
03:08 And even as Jannes and Jambres withstood Moses, so do these also withstand the truth; men corrupted in mind, reprobate concerning the faith.
03:09 But they shall proceed no further: for their folly shall be evident unto all men, as theirs also came to be.
03:10 But thou hast followed my teaching, conduct, purpose, faith, long-suffering, love, patience,
03:11 persecutions, sufferings; what things befell me at Antioch, at Iconium, at Lystra; what persecutions I endured: and out of them all the Lord delivered me.
03:12 Yea, and all that would live godly in Christ Jesus shall suffer persecution.
03:13 But evil men and impostors shall wax worse and worse, deceiving and being deceived.
03:14 But abide thou in the things which thou hast learned and hast been assured of, knowing of whom thou hast learned them;
03:15 and that from a babe thou hast known the sacred writings which are able to make thee wise unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus.
03:16 Every scripture [is] inspired of God, and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, for instruction which is in righteousness:
03:17 that the man of God may be complete, furnished completely unto every good work.
04:01 I charge [thee] therefore in the sight of God, and of the Lord Jesus Christ, who shall judge the living and the dead at his appearing and his kingdom:
04:02 preach the word; be urgent in season, out of season; reprove, rebuke, exhort, with all long-suffering and teaching.
04:03 For the time will come when they will not endure the sound doctrine; but, having itching ears, will heap to themselves teachers after their own lusts;
04:04 and will turn away their ears from the truth, and turn aside unto fables.
04:05 But be thou sober in all things, suffer hardship, do the work of an evangelist, fulfill thy ministry.
04:06 For I am already being offered, and the time of my departure is come.
04:07 I have fought the good fight, I have finished the course, I have kept the faith:
04:08 henceforth there is laid up for me the crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous judge, shall give to me at that day; and not to me only, but also to all them that have loved his appearing.
04:09 Give diligence to come shortly unto me:
04:10 for Demas forsook me, having loved this present world, and went to Thessalonica; Crescens to Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia.
04:11 Only Luke is with me. Take Mark, and bring him with thee; for he is useful to me for ministering.
04:12 But Tychicus I sent to Ephesus.
04:13 The cloak that I left at Troas with Carpus, bring when thou comest, and the books, especially the parchments.
04:14 Alexander the coppersmith did me much evil: the Lord render to him according to his works:
04:15 of whom do thou also beware; for he hath greatly withstood our words.
04:16 At my first defense no one took my part, but all forsook me: may it not be laid to their account.
04:17 But the Lord stood by me, and strengthened me; that through me the message might be fully proclaimed, and that all the Gentiles might hear: and I was delivered out of the mouth of the lion.
04:18 And the Lord will deliver me from every evil work, and will save me unto his heavenly kingdom: to whom [be] the glory forever and ever. Amen.
04:19 Salute Prisca and Aquila, and the house of Onesiphorus.
04:20 Erastus remained at Corinth: but Trophimus I left at Miletus sick.
04:21 Give diligence to come before winter. Eubulus saluteth thee, and Pudens, and Linus, and Claudia, and all the brethren.
04:22 The Lord Jesus Christ be with thy spirit. Grace be with you. Amen.
