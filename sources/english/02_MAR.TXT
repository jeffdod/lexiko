01:01 The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God.
01:02 As it is written in the prophets, Behold, I send my messenger before thy face, Who shall prepare thy way before thee;
01:03 The voice of one crying in the wilderness, Make ye ready the way of the Lord, Make his paths straight;
01:04 John came baptizing in the wilderness, and preaching the baptism of repentance unto remission of sins.
01:05 And there went out unto him all the country of Judea, and they of Jerusalem; and they were all baptized of him in the river Jordan, confessing their sins.
01:06 Now John was clothed with camel's hair, and [had] a leathern girdle about his loins, and did eat locusts and wild honey.
01:07 And he preached, saying, There cometh after me he that is mightier than I, the latchet of whose shoes I am not worthy to stoop down and unloose.
01:08 I indeed baptized you in water; but he shall baptize you in the Holy Spirit.
01:09 And it came to pass in those days, that Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee, and was baptized of John in the Jordan.
01:10 And straightaway coming up from the water, he saw the heavens rent asunder, and the Spirit as a dove descending upon him:
01:11 and a voice came out of the heavens, Thou art my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.
01:12 And straightaway the Spirit driveth him forth into the wilderness.
01:13 And he was there in the wilderness forty days tempted of Satan; and he was with the wild beasts; and the angels ministered unto him.
01:14 Now after John was delivered up, Jesus came into Galilee, preaching the gospel of the kingdom of God,
01:15 and saying, The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand: repent ye, and believe in the gospel.
01:16 And walking by the sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew the brother of the same Simon, casting a net in the sea; for they were fishers.
01:17 And Jesus said unto them, Come ye after me, and I will make you to become fishers of men.
01:18 And straightaway they left their nets, and followed him.
01:19 And going on a little further thence, he saw James the [son] of Zebedee, and John his brother, who also were in the boat mending the nets.
01:20 And straightaway he called them: and they left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired servants, and went after him.
01:21 And they go into Capernaum; and straightaway on the sabbath day he entered into the synagogue and taught.
01:22 And they were astonished at his teaching: for he taught them as having authority, and not as the scribes.
01:23 And there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit; and he cried out,
01:24 saying, Let [us] alone; what have we to do with thee, Jesus thou Nazarene? art thou come to destroy us? I know thee who thou art, the Holy One of God.
01:25 And Jesus rebuked him, saying, Hold thy peace, and come out of him.
01:26 And the unclean spirit, tearing him and crying with a loud voice, came out of him.
01:27 And they were all amazed, insomuch that they questioned among themselves, saying, What is this? what new teaching is this, that with authority he commandeth even the unclean spirits, and they obey him?
01:28 And the report of him went out straightaway into all the region of Galilee round about.
01:29 And straightaway, when they were come out of the synagogue, they came into the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John.
01:30 Now Simon's wife's mother lay sick of a fever; and straightaway they tell him of her:
01:31 and he came and took her by her hand, and raised her up; and straightaway the fever left her, and she ministered unto them.
01:32 And at even, when the sun did set, they brought unto him all that were sick, and them that were possessed with demons.
01:33 And all the city was gathered together at the door.
01:34 And he healed many that were sick with diverse diseases, and cast out many demons; and he suffered not the demons to speak, because they knew him.
01:35 And in the morning, a great while before day, he rose up and went out, and departed into a desert place, and there prayed.
01:36 And Simon and they that were with him followed after him;
01:37 and when they had found him, they say unto him, All are seeking thee.
01:38 And he saith unto them, Let us go into the next towns, that I may preach there also; for to this end came I forth.
01:39 And he was preaching in their synagogues throughout all Galilee, and casting out demons.
01:40 And there cometh to him a leper, beseeching him, and kneeling down to him, and saying unto him, If thou wilt, thou canst make me clean.
01:41 And Jesus, being moved with compassion, stretched forth his hand, and touched him, and saith unto him, I will; be thou made clean.
01:42 And when he had spoken, straightaway the leprosy departed from him, and he was made clean.
01:43 And he strictly charged him, and straightaway sent him out,
01:44 and saith unto him, See thou say nothing to any man: but go show thyself to the priest, and offer for thy cleansing the things which Moses commanded, for a testimony unto them.
01:45 But he went out, and began to publish it much, and to spread abroad the matter, insomuch that Jesus could no more openly enter into a city, but was without in desert places: and they came to him from every quarter.
02:01 And he entered again into Capernaum after some days; and it was noised that he was in the house.
02:02 And straightaway many were gathered together, so that there was no longer room [for them], no, not even about the door: and he spake the word unto them.
02:03 And they come, bringing unto him a man sick of the palsy, borne of four.
02:04 And when they could not come nigh unto him for the crowd, they uncovered the roof where he was: and when they had broken it up, they let down the bed whereon the sick of the palsy lay.
02:05 And Jesus seeing their faith saith unto the sick of the palsy, Son, thy sins are forgiven thee.
02:06 But there were certain of the scribes sitting there, and reasoning in their hearts,
02:07 Why doth this man thus speak blasphemies? who can forgive sins but one, [even] God?
02:08 And straightaway Jesus, perceiving in his spirit that they so reasoned within themselves, said unto them, Why reason ye these things in your hearts?
02:09 Which is easier, to say to the sick of the palsy, Thy sins are forgiven; or to say, Arise, and take up thy bed, and walk?
02:10 But that ye may know that the Son of man hath authority on earth to forgive sins (he saith to the sick of the palsy),
02:11 I say unto thee, Arise, and take up thy bed, and go unto thy house.
02:12 And straightaway he arose, and took up the bed, and went forth before them all; insomuch that they were all amazed, and glorified God, saying, We never saw it on this fashion.
02:13 And he went forth again by the seaside; and all the multitude resorted unto him, and he taught them.
02:14 And as he passed by, he saw Levi the [son] of Alphaeus sitting at the place of toll, and he saith unto him, Follow me. And he arose and followed him.
02:15 And it came to pass, as he was sitting at meat in his house, that many publicans and sinners sat down with Jesus and his disciples: for there were many, and they followed him.
02:16 And the scribes and the Pharisees, when they saw him eating with the publicans and sinners, said unto his disciples, How [is it] that he eateth and drinketh with publicans and sinners?
02:17 And when Jesus heard it, he saith unto them, They that are whole have no need of a physician, but they that are sick: I came not to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.
02:18 And the disciples of John and those of the Pharisees were fasting: and they come and say unto him, Why do the disciples of John and those of the Pharisees fast, but thy disciples fast not?
02:19 And Jesus said unto them, Can the sons of the bridechamber fast, while the bridegroom is with them? as long as they have the bridegroom with them, they cannot fast.
02:20 But the days will come, when the bridegroom shall be taken away from them, and then will they fast in those days.
02:21 And no man seweth a piece of undressed cloth on an old garment: else the new piece that filled it up taketh away from the old, and a worse rent is made.
02:22 And no man putteth new wine into old wineskins; else the new wine doth burst the skins, and the wine is spilled, and the skins will perish: but new wine must be put into fresh wineskins.
02:23 And it came to pass, that he was going on the sabbath day through the grainfields; and his disciples began, as they went, to pluck the ears.
02:24 And the Pharisees said unto him, Behold, why do they on the sabbath day that which is not lawful?
02:25 And he said unto them, Did ye never read what David did, when he had need, and was hungry, he, and they that were with him?
02:26 How he entered into the house of God in the days of Abiathar the high priest, and ate the showbread, which it is not lawful to eat save for the priests, and gave also to them that were with him?
02:27 And he said unto them, The sabbath was made for man, not man for the sabbath:
02:28 so that the Son of man is lord even of the sabbath.
03:01 And he entered again into the synagogue; and there was a man there who had his hand withered.
03:02 And they watched him, whether he would heal him on the sabbath day; that they might accuse him.
03:03 And he saith unto the man that had his hand withered, Stand forth.
03:04 And he saith unto them, Is it lawful on the sabbath day to do good, or to do harm? to save a life, or to kill? But they held their peace.
03:05 And when he had looked round about on them with anger, being grieved at the hardening of their heart, he saith unto the man, Stretch forth thy hand. And he stretched it forth; and his hand was restored whole, as the other.
03:06 And the Pharisees went out, and straightaway with the Herodians took counsel against him, how they might destroy him.
03:07 And Jesus withdrew with his disciples to the sea: and a great multitude from Galilee followed him, and from Judea,
03:08 and from Jerusalem, and from Idumea, and beyond the Jordan, and they about Tyre and Sidon, a great multitude, when they heard what great things he did, came unto him.
03:09 And he spake to his disciples, that a little boat should wait on him because of the crowd, lest they should throng him:
03:10 for he had healed many; insomuch that as many as had plagues pressed upon him that they might touch him.
03:11 And the unclean spirits, whensoever they beheld him, fell down before him, and cried, saying, Thou art the Son of God.
03:12 And he charged them much that they should not make him known.
03:13 And he goeth up into the mountain, and calleth unto him whom he himself would; and they went unto him.
03:14 And he appointed twelve, that they might be with him, and that he might send them forth to preach,
03:15 and to have authority to heal diseases, and to cast out demons:
03:16 and Simon he surnamed Peter;
03:17 and James the [son] of Zebedee, and John the brother of James; and them he surnamed Boanerges, which is, Sons of thunder:
03:18 and Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James the [son] of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus, and Simon the Cananite,
03:19 and Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him. And they come into a house.
03:20 And a multitude cometh together again, so that they could not so much as eat bread.
03:21 And when his friends heard it, they went out to lay hold on him: for they said, He is beside himself.
03:22 And the scribes that came down from Jerusalem said, He hath Beelzebul, and, By the prince of demons casteth he out demons.
03:23 And he called them unto him, and said unto them in parables, How can Satan cast out Satan?
03:24 And if a kingdom be divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand.
03:25 And if a house be divided against itself, that house cannot stand.
03:26 And if Satan hath risen up against himself, and is divided, he cannot stand, but hath an end.
03:27 No one can enter into the house of the strong [man], and plunder his goods, except he first bind the strong [man]; and then he may plunder his house.
03:28 Verily I say unto you, All their sins shall be forgiven unto the sons of men, and blasphemies wherewith soever they shall blaspheme:
03:29 but whosoever shall blaspheme against the Holy Spirit hath never forgiveness, but is in danger of eternal judgment:
03:30 because they said, He hath an unclean spirit.
03:31 There come then his brethren and his mother; and, standing without, they sent unto him, calling him.
03:32 And a multitude was sitting about him; and they said unto him, Behold, thy mother, and thy brethren, and thy sisters without seek for thee.
03:33 And he answered them, saying, Who is my mother or my brethren?
03:34 And looking round on them that sat round about him, he saith, Behold, my mother and my brethren!
03:35 For whosoever shall do the will of God, the same is my brother, and my sister, and mother.
04:01 And again he began to teach by the seaside. And there was gathered unto him a great multitude, so that he entered into the boat, and sat in the sea; and all the multitude was by the sea on the land.
04:02 And he taught them many things in parables, and said unto them in his teaching,
04:03 Hearken: Behold, the sower went forth to sow:
04:04 and it came to pass, as he sowed, some [seed] fell by the wayside, and the birds came and devoured it.
04:05 And other fell on the rocky [ground], where it had not much earth; and straightaway it sprang up, because it had no deepness of earth:
04:06 but when the sun was risen, it was scorched; and because it had no root, it withered away.
04:07 And other fell among the thorns, and the thorns grew up, and choked it, and it yielded no fruit.
04:08 And other fell into the good ground, and yielded fruit, growing up and increasing; and brought forth, thirtyfold, and sixtyfold, and a hundredfold.
04:09 And he said, He that hath ears to hear, let him hear.
04:10 And when he was alone, they that were about him with the twelve asked of him the parable.
04:11 And he said unto them, Unto you is given to know the mystery of the kingdom of God: but unto them that are without, all things are done in parables:
04:12 that seeing they may see, and not perceive; and hearing they may hear, and not understand; lest haply they should turn again, and their sins should be forgiven them.
04:13 And he saith unto them, Know ye not this parable? and how shall ye know all the parables?
04:14 The sower soweth the word.
04:15 And these are they by the wayside, where the word is sown; and when they have heard, straightaway cometh Satan, and taketh away the word which hath been sown in their hearts.
04:16 And these in like manner are they that are sown upon the rocky [places], who, when they have heard the word, straightaway receive it with joy;
04:17 and they have no root in themselves, but endure for a while; then, when tribulation or persecution ariseth because of the word, straightaway they stumble.
04:18 And these are they that are sown among the thorns; such as hear the word,
04:19 and the cares of this world, and the deceitfulness of riches, and the lusts of other things entering in, choke the word, and it becometh unfruitful.
04:20 And these are they that were sown upon the good ground; such as hear the word, and accept it, and bear fruit, thirtyfold, and sixtyfold, and a hundredfold.
04:21 And he said unto them, Is the lamp brought to be put under the bushel, or under the bed, [and] not to be put on the stand?
04:22 For there is not anything hidden, which shall not be manifested; neither was [anything] made secret, but that it should come to light.
04:23 If any man hath ears to hear, let him hear.
04:24 And he said unto them, Take heed what ye hear: with what measure ye mete it shall be measured unto you; and more shall be given unto you that hear.
04:25 For whoso hath, to him shall be given: and he that hath not, from him shall be taken away even that which he hath.
04:26 And he said, So is the kingdom of God, as if a man should cast seed upon the earth;
04:27 and should sleep and rise night and day, and the seed should spring up and grow, he knoweth not how.
04:28 For the earth beareth fruit of herself; first the blade, then the ear, then the full grain in the ear.
04:29 But when the fruit is ripe, straightaway he putteth forth the sickle, because the harvest is come.
04:30 And he said, Whereunto shall we liken the kingdom of God? or with what comparison shall we compare it?
04:31 It is like a grain of mustard seed, which, when it is sown upon the earth, is less than all the seeds that are upon the earth,
04:32 yet when it is sown, groweth up, and becometh greater than all the herbs, and putteth out great branches; so that the birds of the heaven can lodge under the shadow thereof.
04:33 And with many such parables spake he the word unto them, as they were able to hear it;
04:34 and without a parable spake he not unto them: but privately to his disciples he expounded all things.
04:35 And on that day, when even was come, he saith unto them, Let us go over unto the other side.
04:36 And leaving the multitude, they take him with them, even as he was, in the boat. And other little boats also were with him.
04:37 And there ariseth a great storm of wind, and the waves beat into the boat, insomuch that it was now filling.
04:38 And he himself was in the stern, asleep on the cushion: and they awake him, and say unto him, Teacher, carest thou not that we perish?
04:39 And he awoke, and rebuked the wind, and said unto the sea, Peace, be still. And the wind ceased, and there was a great calm.
04:40 And he said unto them, Why are ye so fearful? how is it that ye have no faith?
04:41 And they feared exceedingly, and said one to another, Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?
05:01 And they came to the other side of the sea, into the country of the Gadarenes.
05:02 And when he was come out of the boat, straightaway there met him out of the tombs a man with an unclean spirit,
05:03 who had his dwelling in the tombs: and no man could bind him, no, not with a chain;
05:04 because that he had been often bound with fetters and chains, and the chains had been rent asunder by him, and the fetters broken in pieces: and no man had strength to tame him.
05:05 And always, night and day, in the mountains and in the tombs, he was crying out, and cutting himself with stones.
05:06 But when he saw Jesus from afar, he ran and worshiped him;
05:07 and crying out with a loud voice, he said, What have I to do with thee, Jesus, thou Son of the Most High God? I adjure thee by God, torment me not.
05:08 For he said unto him, Come forth, thou unclean spirit, out of the man.
05:09 And he asked him, What is thy name? And he answered, saying, My name is Legion; for we are many.
05:10 And he besought him much that he would not send them away out of the country.
05:11 Now there was there on the mountainside a great herd of swine feeding.
05:12 And all the demons besought him, saying, Send us into the swine, that we may enter into them.
05:13 And straightaway Jesus gave them leave. And the unclean spirits came out, and entered into the swine: and the herd rushed down the steep into the sea (and they were about two thousand); and they were drowned in the sea.
05:14 And they that fed the swine fled, and told it in the city, and in the country. And they came out to see what it was that had come to pass.
05:15 And they come to Jesus, and behold him that was possessed with demons sitting, and clothed, and in his right mind, [even] him that had the legion: and they were afraid.
05:16 And they that saw it declared unto them how it befell him that was possessed with demons, and concerning the swine.
05:17 And they began to beseech him to depart from their borders.
05:18 And when he was entered into the boat, he that had been possessed with demons besought him that he might be with him.
05:19 But Jesus suffered him not, but saith unto him, Go to thy house unto thy friends, and tell them how great things the Lord hath done for thee, and [how] he had mercy on thee.
05:20 And he went his way, and began to publish in Decapolis how great things Jesus had done for him: and all men marveled.
05:21 And when Jesus had crossed over again in the boat unto the other side, a great multitude was gathered unto him; and he was by the sea.
05:22 And behold, there cometh one of the rulers of the synagogue, Jairus by name; and seeing him, he falleth at his feet,
05:23 and besought him much, saying, My little daughter is at the point of death: [I pray thee], that thou come and lay thy hands on her, that she may be made whole; and she shall live.
05:24 And he went with him; and a great multitude followed him, and they thronged him.
05:25 And a certain woman, who had an issue of blood twelve years,
05:26 and had suffered many things of many physicians, and had spent all that she had, and was nothing bettered, but rather grew worse,
05:27 having heard of Jesus, came in the crowd behind, and touched his garment.
05:28 For she said, If I touch but his garments, I shall be made whole.
05:29 And straightaway the fountain of her blood was dried up; and she felt in her body that she was healed of her plague.
05:30 And straightaway Jesus, perceiving in himself that the power [proceeding] from him had gone forth, turned him about in the crowd, and said, Who touched my garments?
05:31 And his disciples said unto him, Thou seest the multitude thronging thee, and sayest thou, Who touched me?
05:32 And he looked round about to see her that had done this thing.
05:33 But the woman fearing and trembling, knowing what had been done to her, came and fell down before him, and told him all the truth.
05:34 And he said unto her, Daughter, thy faith hath made thee whole; go in peace, and be whole of thy plague.
05:35 While he yet spake, they come from the ruler of the synagogue's [house], saying, Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Teacher any further?
05:36 But straightaway Jesus, when he heard the word that was spoken, saith unto the ruler of the synagogue, Fear not, only believe.
05:37 And he suffered no man to follow him, save Peter, and James, and John the brother of James.
05:38 And he cometh to the house of the ruler of the synagogue; and he beholdeth a tumult, [many] weeping and wailing greatly.
05:39 And when he was entered in, he saith unto them, Why make ye a tumult, and weep? the child is not dead, but sleepeth.
05:40 And they laughed him to scorn. But he, having put them all forth, taketh the father of the child and her mother and them that were with him, and goeth in where the child was lying.
05:41 And taking the child by the hand, he saith unto her, Talitha cumi; which is, being interpreted, Damsel, I say unto thee, Arise.
05:42 And straightaway the damsel rose up, and walked; for she was twelve years old. And they were amazed with a great amazement.
05:43 And he charged them much that no man should know this: and he commanded that [something] should be given her to eat.
06:01 And he went out from thence; and he came into his own country; and his disciples follow him.
06:02 And when the sabbath was come, he began to teach in the synagogue: and many hearing him were astonished, saying, Whence hath this man these things? and, What is the wisdom that is given unto him, [that] even such mighty works are wrought by his hands?
06:03 Is not this the carpenter, the son of Mary, and brother of James, and Joses, and Judas, and Simon? and are not his sisters here with us? And they were offended in him.
06:04 And Jesus said unto them, A prophet is not without honor, save in his own country, and among his own kin, and in his own house.
06:05 And he could there do no mighty work, save that he laid his hands upon a few sick folk, and healed them.
06:06 And he marveled because of their unbelief. And he went round about the villages teaching.
06:07 And he calleth unto him the twelve, and began to send them forth by two and two; and he gave them authority over the unclean spirits;
06:08 and he charged them that they should take nothing for [their] journey, save a staff only; no wallet, no bread, no money in their purse;
06:09 but [to go] shod with sandals: and, [said he], Put not on two coats.
06:10 And he said unto them, Wheresoever ye enter into a house, there abide till ye depart thence.
06:11 And whosoever shall not receive you, nor hear you, as ye go forth thence, shake off the dust that is under your feet for a testimony unto them. Verily I say unto you, It shall be more tolerable for Sodom or Gomorrah in the day of judgment, than for that city.
06:12 And they went out, and preached that [men] should repent.
06:13 And they cast out many demons, and anointed with oil many that were sick, and healed them.
06:14 And king Herod heard [thereof]; for his name had become known: and he said, John the Baptizer is risen from the dead, and therefore do these powers work in him.
06:15 Others said, It is Elijah. And others said, It is a prophet, as one of the prophets.
06:16 But Herod, when he heard [thereof], said, It is John, whom I beheaded: he is risen from the dead.
06:17 For Herod himself had sent forth and laid hold upon John, and bound him in prison for the sake of Herodias, his brother Philip's wife; for he had married her.
06:18 For John said unto Herod, It is not lawful for thee to have thy brother's wife.
06:19 And Herodias set herself against him, and desired to kill him; and she could not;
06:20 for Herod feared John, knowing that he was a righteous and holy man, and kept him safe. And when he heard him, he did many things; and he heard him gladly.
06:21 And when a convenient day was come, that Herod on his birthday made a supper to his lords, and the high captains, and the chief men of Galilee;
06:22 and when the daughter of Herodias herself came in and danced, and pleased Herod and them that sat at meat with him, the king said unto the damsel, Ask of me whatsoever thou wilt, and I will give it thee.
06:23 And he sware unto her, Whatsoever thou shalt ask of me, I will give it thee, unto the half of my kingdom.
06:24 And she went out, and said unto her mother, What shall I ask? And she said, The head of John the Baptist.
06:25 And she came in straightaway with haste unto the king, and asked, saying, I will that thou give me forthwith on a platter the head of John the Baptist.
06:26 And the king was exceeding sorry; but for the sake of his oaths, and of them that sat at meat with him, he would not reject her.
06:27 And straightaway the king sent forth a soldier of his guard, and commanded his head to be brought:
06:28 and he went and beheaded him in the prison, and brought his head on a platter, and gave it to the damsel; and the damsel gave it to her mother.
06:29 And when his disciples heard [thereof], they came and took up his corpse, and laid it in a tomb.
06:30 And the apostles gather themselves together unto Jesus; and they told him all things, even whatsoever they had done, and whatsoever they had taught.
06:31 And he said unto them, Come ye yourselves apart into a desert place, and rest a while. For there were many coming and going, and they had no leisure so much as to eat.
06:32 And they went away to a desert place by boat privately.
06:33 And [the people] saw them going, and many knew him, and they ran together there on foot from all the cities, and outwent them, and came together unto him.
06:34 And Jesus, when he came forth, saw a great multitude, and he had compassion on them, because they were as sheep not having a shepherd: and he began to teach them many things.
06:35 And when the day was now far spent, his disciples having come unto him say, The place is desert, and the day is now far spent;
06:36 send them away, that they may go into the country and villages round about, and buy themselves bread: for they have nothing to eat.
06:37 But he answered and said unto them, Give ye them to eat. And they say unto him, Shall we go and buy two hundred denarii' worth of bread, and give them to eat?
06:38 And he saith unto them, How many loaves have ye? go and see. And when they knew, they say, Five, and two fish.
06:39 And he commanded them to make all sit down by companies upon the green grass.
06:40 And they sat down in ranks, by hundreds, and by fifties.
06:41 And he took the five loaves and the two fish, and looking up to heaven, he blessed, and brake the loaves; and he gave to his disciples to set before them; and the two fish divided he among them all.
06:42 And they all ate, and were filled.
06:43 And they took up twelve baskets full of the broken pieces, and of the fish.
06:44 And they that ate the loaves were five thousand men.
06:45 And straightaway he constrained his disciples to enter into the boat, and to go before [him] unto the other side to Bethsaida, while he himself sent the multitude away.
06:46 And after he had taken leave of them, he departed into the mountain to pray.
06:47 And when even was come, the boat was in the midst of the sea, and he alone on the land.
06:48 And he saw them distressed in rowing, for the wind was contrary unto them: and about the fourth watch of the night he cometh unto them, walking on the sea; and he would have passed by them:
06:49 but they, when they saw him walking on the sea, supposed it to be a ghost, and cried out;
06:50 for they all saw him, and were troubled. And straightaway he spake with them, and saith unto them, Be of good cheer: it is I; be not afraid.
06:51 And he went up unto them into the boat; and the wind ceased: and they were sore amazed in themselves beyond measure, and marveled;
06:52 for they understood not concerning the loaves, for their heart was hardened.

12:27 He is not the God of the dead, but the God of the living: ye therefore do greatly err.
12:28 And one of the scribes came, and heard them questioning together, and knowing that he had answered them well, asked him, Which is the first commandment of all?
12:29 And Jesus answered him, The first of all the commandments [is], Hear, O Israel; The Lord our God, the Lord is one:
12:30 and thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind, and with all thy strength: this is the first commandment.
12:31 And a second like it [is] this, Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself. There is none other commandment greater than these.
12:32 And the scribe said unto him, Of a truth, Teacher, thou hast well said that he is one; and there is none other but he:
12:33 and to love him with all the heart, and with all the understanding, and with all the soul, and with all the strength, and to love his neighbor as himself, is more than all whole burnt offerings and sacrifices.
12:34 And when Jesus saw that he answered discreetly, he said unto him, Thou art not far from the kingdom of God. And no man after that durst ask him any question.
12:35 And Jesus answered and said, as he taught in the temple, How say the scribes that the Christ is the son of David?
12:36 for David himself said in the Holy Spirit, The Lord saith unto my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand, Till I make thine enemies the footstool of thy feet.
12:37 David himself therefore calleth him Lord; and whence is he his son? And the common people heard him gladly.
12:38 And he said unto them in his teaching, Beware of the scribes, who desire to walk in long robes, and [to have] salutations in the marketplaces,
12:39 and chief seats in the synagogues, and chief places at feasts:
12:40 they that devour widows' houses, and for a pretense make long prayers; these shall receive greater condemnation.
12:41 And Jesus sat down over against the treasury, and beheld how the multitude cast money into the treasury: and many that were rich cast in much.
12:42 And there came a poor widow, and she cast in two mites, which make a penny.
12:43 And he called unto him his disciples, and saith unto them, Verily I say unto you, This poor widow hath cast in more than all they that are casting into the treasury:
12:44 for they all did cast in of their superfluity; but she of her want did cast in all that she had, [even] all her living.
13:01 And as he went forth out of the temple, one of his disciples saith unto him, Teacher, behold, what manner of stones and what manner of buildings!
13:02 And Jesus answered and said unto him, Seest thou these great buildings? there shall not be left one stone upon another, which shall not be thrown down.
13:03 And as he sat on the mount of Olives over against the temple, Peter and James and John and Andrew asked him privately,
13:04 Tell us, when shall these things be? and what [shall be] the sign when all these things are about to be accomplished?
13:05 And Jesus answered them and began to say, Take heed that no man lead you astray.
13:06 For many shall come in my name, saying, I am [he]; and shall lead many astray.
13:07 And when ye shall hear of wars and rumors of wars, be not troubled: for [these things] must needs come to pass; but the end is not yet.
13:08 For nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom; and there shall be earthquakes in diverse places; and there shall be famines and troubles: these things are the beginnings of travail.
13:09 But take ye heed to yourselves: for they shall deliver you up to councils; and in synagogues shall ye be beaten; and before governors and kings shall ye stand for my sake, for a testimony unto them.
13:10 And the gospel must first be preached unto all the nations.
13:11 But when they shall lead you [to judgment], and deliver you up, be not anxious beforehand what ye shall speak, neither do ye premeditate: but whatsoever shall be given you in that hour, that speak ye; for it is not ye that speak, but the Holy Spirit.
13:12 And brother shall deliver up brother to death, and the father his child; and children shall rise up against parents, and cause them to be put to death.
13:13 And ye shall be hated of all men for my name's sake: but he that endureth to the end, the same shall be saved.
13:14 But when ye see the abomination of desolation, which was spoken of by Daniel the prophet, standing where he ought not (let him that readeth understand), then let them that are in Judea flee unto the mountains:
13:15 and let him that is on the housetop not go down to the house, nor enter in, to take anything out of his house:
13:16 and let him that is in the field not return back to take his cloak.
13:17 But woe unto them that are with child and to them that give suck in those days!
13:18 And pray ye that your flight be not in the winter.
13:19 For those days shall be tribulation, such as there hath not been the like from the beginning of the creation which God created until now, and never shall be.
13:20 And except the Lord had shortened the days, no flesh would have been saved; but for the elect's sake, whom he chose, he shortened the days.
13:21 Then if any man shall say unto you, Lo, here is the Christ; or, Lo, there; believe [it] not:
13:22 for there shall arise false Christs and false prophets, and shall show signs and wonders, that they may lead astray, if possible, even the elect.
13:23 But take ye heed: behold, I have told you all things beforehand.
13:24 But in those days, after that tribulation, the sun shall be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light,
13:25 and the stars of heaven shall be falling, and the powers that are in the heavens shall be shaken.
13:26 And then shall they see the Son of man coming in clouds with great power and glory.
13:27 And then shall he send forth his angels, and shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from the uttermost part of the earth to the uttermost part of heaven.
13:28 Now from the fig tree learn her parable: when her branch is now become tender, and putteth forth its leaves, ye know that the summer is nigh;
13:29 even so ye also, when ye see these things coming to pass, know ye that he is nigh, [even] at the doors.
13:30 Verily I say unto you, This generation shall not pass away, until all these things be accomplished.
13:31 Heaven and earth shall pass away: but my words shall not pass away.
13:32 But of that day or hour knoweth no one, not even the angels that are in heaven, neither the Son, but the Father.
13:33 Take ye heed, watch and pray: for ye know not when the time is.
13:34 [It is] as [when] a man, sojourning in another country, having left his house, and given authority to his servants, and to each one his work, commanded also the porter to watch.
13:35 Watch therefore: for ye know not when the lord of the house cometh, at even, or at midnight, or at cockcrowing, or in the morning;
13:36 lest coming suddenly he find you sleeping.
13:37 And what I say unto you I say unto all, Watch.
14:01 Now after two days was [the feast of] the passover and the unleavened bread: and the chief priests and the scribes sought how they might take him with subtlety, and kill him:
14:02 but they said, Not during the feast, lest haply there shall be a tumult of the people.
14:03 And while he was in Bethany in the house of Simon the leper, as he sat at meat, there came a woman having an alabaster cruse of ointment of pure nard very costly; and she brake the cruse, and poured it over his head.
14:04 But there were some that had indignation among themselves, and saying, To what purpose hath this waste of the ointment been made?
14:05 For this might have been sold for above three hundred denarii, and given to the poor. And they murmured against her.
14:06 But Jesus said, Let her alone; why trouble ye her? she hath wrought a good work on me.
14:07 For ye have the poor always with you, and whensoever ye will ye can do them good: but me ye have not always.
14:08 She hath done what she could; she hath anointed my body beforehand for the burying.
14:09 Verily I say unto you, Wheresoever this gospel shall be preached throughout the whole world, that also which this woman hath done shall be spoken of for a memorial of her.
14:10 And Judas Iscariot, one of the twelve, went away unto the chief priests, that he might deliver him unto them.
14:11 And they, when they heard it, were glad, and promised to give him money. And he sought how he might conveniently deliver him [unto them].
14:12 And on the first day of unleavened bread, when they sacrificed the passover, his disciples say unto him, Where wilt thou that we go and make ready that thou mayest eat the passover?
14:13 And he sendeth two of his disciples, and saith unto them, Go into the city, and there shall meet you a man bearing a pitcher of water: follow him;
14:14 and wheresoever he shall enter in, say to the master of the house, The Teacher saith, Where is the guestchamber, where I shall eat the passover with my disciples?
14:15 And he will himself show you a large upper room furnished [and] ready: there make ready for us.
14:16 And his disciples went forth, and came into the city, and found as he had said unto them: and they made ready the passover.
14:17 And when it was evening he cometh with the twelve.
14:18 And as they sat and were eating, Jesus said, Verily I say unto you, One of you shall betray me, [even] he that eateth with me.
14:19 And they began to be sorrowful, and to say unto him one by one, Is it I? and another, Is it I?
14:20 And he answered and said unto them, [It is] one of the twelve, he that dippeth with me in the dish.
14:21 The Son of man indeed goeth, even as it is written of him: but woe unto that man through whom the Son of man is betrayed! good were it for that man if he had not been born.
14:22 And as they were eating, Jesus took bread, and when he had blessed, he brake it, and gave to them, and said, Take, eat: this is my body.
14:23 And he took the cup, and when he had given thanks, he gave to them: and they all drank of it.
14:24 And he said unto them, This is my blood of the new covenant, which is poured out for many.
14:25 Verily I say unto you, I shall no more drink of the fruit of the vine, until that day when I drink it new in the kingdom of God.
14:26 And when they had sung a hymn, they went out unto the mount of Olives.
14:27 And Jesus saith unto them, All ye shall be offended in me this night: for it is written, I will smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered abroad.
14:28 Howbeit, after I am raised up, I will go before you into Galilee.
14:29 But Peter said unto him, Although all shall be offended, yet will not I.
14:30 And Jesus saith unto him, Verily I say unto thee, that thou today, [even] in this night, before the cock crow twice, shalt deny me thrice.
14:31 But he spake the more vehemently, If I must die with thee, I will not deny thee. And in like manner also said they all.
14:32 And they come unto a place which was named Gethsemane: and he saith unto his disciples, Sit ye here, while I pray.
14:33 And he taketh with him Peter and James and John, and began to be greatly amazed, and sore troubled.
14:34 And he saith unto them, My soul is exceeding sorrowful even unto death: abide ye here, and watch.

14:38 Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.

14:40 And when he returned, he found them sleeping again, for their eyes were heavy; and they knew not what to answer him.
14:41 And he cometh the third time, and saith unto them, Sleep on now, and take your rest: it is enough; the hour is come; behold, the Son of man is betrayed into the hands of sinners.
14:42 Arise, let us be going: behold, he that betrayeth me is at hand.

15:03 And the chief priests accused him of many things.

15:05 But Jesus no more answered anything; insomuch that Pilate marveled.
