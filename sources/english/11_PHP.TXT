01:01 Paul and Timothy, servants of Jesus Christ, to all the saints in Christ Jesus that are at Philippi, with the bishops and deacons:
01:02 Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
01:03 I thank my God upon all my remembrance of you,
01:04 always in every supplication of mine on behalf of you all making my supplication with joy,
01:05 for your fellowship in furtherance of the gospel from the first day until now;
01:06 being confident of this very thing, that he who began a good work in you will perfect it until the day of Christ Jesus:
01:07 even as it is right for me to be thus minded on behalf of you all, because I have you in my heart, inasmuch as, both in my bonds and in the defense and confirmation of the gospel, ye all are partakers with me of grace.
01:08 For God is my witness, how I long after you all in the tender mercies of Jesus Christ.
01:09 And this I pray, that your love may abound yet more and more in knowledge and all discernment;
01:10 so that ye may approve the things that are excellent; that ye may be sincere and void of offense unto the day of Christ;
01:11 being filled with the fruits of righteousness, which are through Jesus Christ, unto the glory and praise of God.
01:12 Now I would have you know, brethren, that the things [which happened] unto me have fallen out rather unto the progress of the gospel;
01:13 so that my bonds became manifest in Christ throughout the whole praetorian guard, and to all the rest;
01:14 and that most of the brethren in the Lord, being confident through my bonds, are more abundantly bold to speak the word without fear.
01:15 Some indeed preach Christ even of envy and strife; and some also of good will:
01:16 the one proclaim Christ of faction, not sincerely, thinking to add affliction to my bonds;
01:17 but the other [do it] of love, knowing that I am set for the defense of the gospel.
01:18 What then? only [that] in every way, whether in pretense or in truth, Christ is proclaimed; and therein I rejoice, yea, and will rejoice.
01:19 For I know that this shall turn out to my salvation, through your supplication and the supply of the Spirit of Jesus Christ,
01:20 according to my earnest expectation and hope, that in nothing shall I be put to shame, but [that] with all boldness, as always, [so] now also Christ shall be magnified in my body, whether by life, or by death.
01:21 For to me to live is Christ, and to die is gain.
01:22 But if to live in the flesh [be my lot], this is the fruit of my work: and what I shall choose I know not.
01:23 But I am in a strait betwixt the two, having the desire to depart and be with Christ; which is very far better:
01:24 yet to abide in the flesh is more needful for your sake.
01:25 And having this confidence, I know that I shall abide, yea, and abide with you all, for your progress and joy in the faith;
01:26 that your glorying may abound in Christ Jesus in me through my presence with you again.
01:27 Only behave as citizens worthily of the gospel of Christ: that, whether I come and see you or be absent, I may hear of your state, that ye stand fast in one spirit, with one soul striving for the faith of the gospel;
01:28 and in nothing affrighted by the adversaries: which is for them an evident token of perdition, but for you of salvation, and that from God;
01:29 because to you it hath been granted in behalf of Christ, not only to believe on him, but also to suffer in his behalf:
01:30 having the same conflict which ye saw in me, and now hear to be in me.
02:01 If there is therefore any exhortation in Christ, if any consolation of love, if any fellowship of the Spirit, if any tender mercies and compassions,
02:02 make full my joy, that ye be of the same mind, having the same love, being of one accord, of one mind;
02:03 [doing] nothing through faction or vainglory, but in lowliness of mind each counting other better than himself;
02:04 look not each of you to his own things, but each of you also to the things of others.
02:05 For let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus:
02:06 who, existing in the form of God, counted not the being on an equality with God a thing to be grasped,
02:07 but emptied himself, taking the form of a servant, being made in the likeness of men;
02:08 and being found in fashion as a man, he humbled himself, becoming obedient [even] unto death, yea, the death of the cross.
02:09 Wherefore also God highly exalted him, and gave unto him a name which is above every name;
02:10 that in the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of [things] in heaven and [things] on earth and [things] under the earth,
02:11 and that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.
02:12 So then, my beloved, even as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling;
02:13 for it is God who worketh in you both to will and to work, for his good pleasure.
02:14 Do all things without murmurings and questionings:
02:15 that ye may become blameless and harmless, children of God without blemish in the midst of a crooked and perverse generation, among whom ye are seen as lights in the world,
02:16 holding forth the word of life; that I may have whereof to glory in the day of Christ, that I did not run in vain neither labor in vain.
02:17 Yea, and if I am offered upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I joy, and rejoice with you all:
02:18 and in the same manner do ye also joy, and rejoice with me.
02:19 But I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy shortly unto you, that I also may be of good comfort, when I know your state.
02:20 For I have no man like-minded, who will care truly for your state.
02:21 For they all seek their own, not the things of Christ Jesus.
02:22 But ye know the proof of him, that, as a child [serveth] a father, [so] he served with me in furtherance of the gospel.
02:23 Him therefore I hope to send forthwith, so soon as I shall see how it will go with me:
02:24 but I trust in the Lord that I myself also shall come shortly.
02:25 But I counted it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus, my brother and fellow worker and fellow soldier, and your messenger and minister to my need;
02:26 since he longed after you all, and was sore troubled, because ye had heard that he was sick:
02:27 for indeed he was sick nigh unto death: but God had mercy on him; and not on him only, but on me also, that I might not have sorrow upon sorrow.
02:28 I have sent him therefore the more diligently, that, when ye see him again, ye may rejoice, and that I may be the less sorrowful.
02:29 Receive him therefore in the Lord with all joy; and hold such in honor:
02:30 because for the work of Christ he came nigh unto death, not regarding his life, to supply that which was lacking in your service toward me.
03:01 Finally, my brethren, rejoice in the Lord. To write the same things to you, to me indeed is not irksome, but for you it is safe.
03:02 Beware of the dogs, beware of the evil workers, beware of the concision:
03:03 for we are the circumcision, who worship by the Spirit of God, and glory in Christ Jesus, and have no confidence in the flesh:
03:04 though I myself might have confidence even in the flesh: if any other man thinketh to have confidence in the flesh, I yet more:
03:05 circumcised the eighth day, of the stock of Israel, of the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew of Hebrews; as touching the law, a Pharisee;
03:06 as touching zeal, persecuting the church; as touching the righteousness which is in the law, found blameless.
03:07 Howbeit what things were gain to me, these have I counted loss for Christ.
03:08 Yea verily, and I count all things to be loss for the excellency of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord: for whom I suffered the loss of all things, and do count them to be but refuse, that I may gain Christ,
03:09 and be found in him, not having a righteousness of mine own, [even] that which is of the law, but that which is through faith in Christ, the righteousness which is from God by faith:
03:10 that I may know him, and the power of his resurrection, and the fellowship of his sufferings, becoming conformed unto his death;
03:11 if by any means I may attain unto the resurrection of the dead.
03:12 Not that I have already obtained, or am already made perfect: but I press on, if so be that I may lay hold on that for which also I was laid hold on by Christ Jesus.
03:13 Brethren, I count not myself to have laid hold: but one thing [I do], forgetting the things which are behind, and stretching forward to the things which are before,
03:14 I press on toward the goal for the prize of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus.
03:15 Let us therefore, as many as are perfect, be thus minded: and if in anything ye are otherwise minded, this also shall God reveal unto you:
03:16 only, whereunto we have attained, by that same rule let us walk, let us be of the same mind.
03:17 Brethren, be ye imitators together of me, and mark them that so walk even as ye have us for an example.
03:18 For many walk, of whom I told you often, and now tell you even weeping, [that they are] the enemies of the cross of Christ:
03:19 whose end is perdition, whose god is the belly, and [whose] glory is in their shame, who mind earthly things.
03:20 For our citizenship is in heaven; whence also we wait for a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ:
03:21 who shall fashion anew the body of our humiliation, that it may be conformed to the body of his glory, according to the working whereby he is able even to subject all things unto himself.
04:01 Wherefore, my brethren beloved and longed for, my joy and crown, so stand fast in the Lord, my beloved.
04:02 I exhort Euodia, and I exhort Syntyche, to be of the same mind in the Lord.
04:03 Yea, I beseech thee also, true yokefellow, help these women, for they labored with me in the gospel, with Clement also, and the rest of my fellow workers, whose names are in the book of life.
04:04 Rejoice in the Lord always: again I will say, Rejoice.
04:05 Let your forbearance be known unto all men. The Lord is at hand.
04:06 In nothing be anxious; but in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God.
04:07 And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus.
04:08 Finally, brethren, whatsoever things are true, whatsoever things are honorable, whatsoever things are just, whatsoever things are pure, whatsoever things are lovely, whatsoever things are of good report; if there be any virtue, and if there be any praise, think on these things.
04:09 The things which ye both learned and received and heard and saw in me, these things do: and the God of peace shall be with you.
04:10 But I rejoice in the Lord greatly, that now at length ye have revived your thought for me; wherein ye did indeed take thought, but ye lacked opportunity.
04:11 Not that I speak in respect of want: for I have learned, in whatsoever state I am, therein to be content.
04:12 I know both how to be abased, and I know how to abound: in everything and in all things have I learned the secret both to be filled and to be hungry, both to abound and to be in want.
04:13 I can do all things through Christ who strengtheneth me.
04:14 Howbeit ye did well that ye had fellowship with my affliction.
04:15 And ye yourselves also know, ye Philippians, that in the beginning of the gospel, when I departed from Macedonia, no church had fellowship with me in the matter of giving and receiving but ye only;
04:16 for even in Thessalonica ye sent once and again unto my need.
04:17 Not that I seek for the gift; but I seek for the fruit that increaseth to your account.
04:18 But I have all things, and abound: I am filled, having received from Epaphroditus the things [that came] from you, an odor of a sweet smell, a sacrifice acceptable, well-pleasing to God.
04:19 And my God shall supply every need of yours according to his riches in glory in Christ Jesus.
04:20 Now unto our God and Father [be] the glory forever and ever. Amen.
04:21 Salute every saint in Christ Jesus. The brethren that are with me salute you.
04:22 All the saints salute you, especially they that are of Caesar's household.
04:23 The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.
