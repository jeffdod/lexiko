01:01 Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother, to Philemon our beloved and fellow worker,
01:02 and to Apphia the beloved, and to Archippus our fellow soldier, and to the church in thy house:
01:03 Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
01:04 I thank my God always, making mention of thee in my prayers,
01:05 hearing of thy love, and of the faith which thou hast toward the Lord Jesus, and toward all the saints;
01:06 that the fellowship of thy faith may become effectual, in the knowledge of every good thing which is in us, unto Christ Jesus.
01:07 For we have much thankfulness and comfort in thy love, because the hearts of the saints have been refreshed through thee, brother.
01:08 Wherefore, though I have all boldness in Christ to enjoin thee that which is befitting,
01:09 yet for love's sake I rather beseech, being such a one as Paul the aged, and now a prisoner also of Jesus Christ:
01:10 I beseech thee for my child, whom I have begotten in my bonds, Onesimus,
01:11 who once was unprofitable to thee, but now is profitable to thee and to me: whom I have sent again:
01:12 and do thou receive him, that is, my very heart:
01:13 whom I would fain have kept with me, that in thy behalf he might minister unto me in the bonds of the gospel:
01:14 but without thy mind I would do nothing; that thy goodness should not be as of necessity, but of free will.
01:15 For perhaps he was therefore parted [from thee] for a season, that thou shouldest have him forever;
01:16 no longer as a servant, but more than a servant, a brother beloved, especially to me, but how much rather to thee, both in the flesh and in the Lord.
01:17 If then thou countest me a partner, receive him as myself.
01:18 But if he hath wronged thee at all, or oweth [thee] aught, put that to mine account;
01:19 I Paul write it with mine own hand, I will repay it: that I say not unto thee that thou owest to me even thine own self besides.
01:20 Yea, brother, let me have joy of thee in the Lord: refresh my heart in the Lord.
01:21 Having confidence in thine obedience I write unto thee, knowing that thou wilt do even beyond what I say.
01:22 But withal prepare me also a lodging: for I hope that through your prayers I shall be granted unto you.
01:23 There salute thee Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus;
01:24 Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, Luke, my fellow workers.
01:25 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen.
