English text for the reader: the American Standard Version Byzantine Text.

Files: NN_XXX.TXT, one per New Testament book in canonical order. One verse per line, "CC:VV text".
UTF-8 with a byte-order mark, Windows line endings. [Square brackets] mark words supplied in English
(the ASV's italics); the reader shows them in italics without the brackets.

Source: supplied by the project owner, who works with the editor, Adam Boyd, in November 2026.
The ASV is in the public domain because its copyright has expired. Adam Boyd dedicated his changes to the
public domain in 2021 (https://ebible.org/engasvbt/copyright.htm).

This copy follows the Robinson-Pierpont verse divisions and includes later corrections by Adam Boyd, so it
differs from the eBible edition in places. Compared with the eBible text dated 31 October 2026, 159 of the
7,953 verses differ in wording, punctuation or capitalization, and a phrase sits in the neighboring verse in
six places (Matthew 26:60-61, Mark 3:19-20, Mark 12:14-15, John 7:21-22, Colossians 1:21-22,
1 Thessalonians 2:11-12), where the RP Greek puts it. 3 John has 14 verses here, as in RP; eBible has 15.

Seven verses have no Greek in RP and so no English here: Luke 17:36, Acts 8:37, 15:34 and 24:7, and
Romans 16:25-27 (the doxology stands at Romans 14:24-26 in both texts).

To rebuild the data the reader uses: python3 tools/build_english.py (after tools/build_data.py).
