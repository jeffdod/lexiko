01:01 The elder unto the elect lady and her children, whom I love in truth; and not I only, but also all they that know the truth;
01:02 for the truth's sake which abideth in us, and it shall be with us forever:
01:03 Grace, mercy, peace shall be with us, from God the Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.
01:04 I rejoice greatly that I have found [certain] of thy children walking in truth, even as we received commandment from the Father.
01:05 And now I beseech thee, lady, not as though writing to thee a new commandment, but that which we had from the beginning, that we love one another.
01:06 And this is love, that we should walk after his commandments. This is the commandment, even as ye heard from the beginning, that ye should walk in it.
01:07 For many deceivers are entered into the world, [even] they that confess not that Jesus Christ cometh in the flesh. This is the deceiver and the antichrist.
01:08 Look to yourselves, that we lose not the things which we have wrought, but that we receive a full reward.
01:09 Whosoever transgresseth and abideth not in the teaching of Christ, hath not God: he that abideth in the teaching of Christ, the same hath both the Father and the Son.
01:10 If anyone cometh unto you, and bringeth not this teaching, receive him not into [your] house, and give him no greeting:
01:11 for he that giveth him greeting partaketh in his evil works.
01:12 Having many things to write unto you, I would not [write them] with paper and ink: but I hope to come unto you, and to speak face to face, that our joy may be made full.
01:13 The children of thine elect sister salute thee. Amen.
