01:01 Paul, a servant of God, and an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to the faith of God's elect, and the knowledge of the truth which is according to godliness,
01:02 in hope of eternal life, which God, who cannot lie, promised before times eternal;
01:03 but in his own seasons manifested his word in the message, wherewith I was entrusted according to the commandment of God our Savior;
01:04 to Titus, my true child after a common faith: Grace, mercy, peace, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ our Savior.
01:05 For this cause left I thee in Crete, that thou shouldest set in order the things that were wanting, and appoint elders in every city, as I gave thee charge;
01:06 if any man is blameless, the husband of one wife, having children that believe, who are not accused of riot or unruly.
01:07 For the bishop must be blameless, as God's steward; not self-willed, not soon angry, no brawler, no striker, not greedy of filthy lucre;
01:08 but given to hospitality, a lover of good, sober-minded, just, holy, self-controlled;
01:09 holding to the faithful word which is according to the teaching, that he may be able both to exhort in the sound doctrine, and to convict the gainsayers.
01:10 For there are many, and they are unruly, vain talkers and deceivers, especially they of the circumcision,
01:11 whose mouths must be stopped; men who overthrow whole houses, teaching things which they ought not, for filthy lucre's sake.
01:12 One of themselves, a prophet of their own, said, Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, idle gluttons.
01:13 This testimony is true. For which cause reprove them sharply, that they may be sound in the faith,
01:14 not giving heed to Jewish fables, and commandments of men who turn away from the truth.
01:15 To the pure all things are indeed pure: but to them that are defiled and unbelieving nothing is pure; but both their mind and their conscience are defiled.
01:16 They profess that they know God; but by their works they deny him, being abominable, and disobedient, and unto every good work reprobate.
02:01 But speak thou the things which befit the sound doctrine:
02:02 that aged men be temperate, grave, sober-minded, sound in faith, in love, in patience:
02:03 that aged women likewise be reverent in demeanor, not slanderers, not enslaved to much wine, teachers of that which is good;
02:04 that they may train the young women to love their husbands, to love their children,
02:05 [to be] sober-minded, chaste, keepers at home, kind, being in subjection to their own husbands, that the word of God be not blasphemed:
02:06 the younger men likewise exhort to be sober-minded:
02:07 in all things showing thyself an example of good works; in thy doctrine [showing] uncorruptness, gravity, incorruptibility,
02:08 sound speech, that cannot be condemned; that he that is of the contrary part may be ashamed, having no evil thing to say of us.
02:09 [Exhort] servants to be in subjection to their own masters, [and] to be well-pleasing [to them] in all things; not gainsaying;
02:10 not purloining, but showing all good fidelity; that they may adorn the doctrine of God our Savior in all things.
02:11 For the saving grace of God hath appeared to all men,
02:12 instructing us, to the intent that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live soberly and righteously and godly in this present world;
02:13 looking for the blessed hope and appearing of the glory of the great God and our Savior Jesus Christ;
02:14 who gave himself for us, that he might redeem us from all iniquity, and purify unto himself a people for his own possession, zealous of good works.
02:15 These things speak and exhort and reprove with all authority. Let no man despise thee.
03:01 Put them in mind to be in subjection to rulers and to authorities, to be obedient, to be ready unto every good work,
03:02 to speak evil of no man, not to be contentious, to be gentle, showing all meekness toward all men.
03:03 For we also once were foolish, disobedient, deceived, serving diverse lusts and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful, hating one another.
03:04 But when the kindness of God our Savior, and his love toward man, appeared,
03:05 not by works [done] in righteousness, which we did ourselves, but according to his mercy he saved us, through the washing of regeneration and renewing of the Holy Spirit,
03:06 which he poured out upon us richly, through Jesus Christ our Savior;
03:07 that, being justified by his grace, we might be made heirs according to the hope of eternal life.
03:08 Faithful is the saying, and concerning these things I desire that thou affirm confidently, to the end that they who have believed God may be careful to maintain good works. These things are good and profitable unto men:
03:09 but shun foolish questionings, and genealogies, and strifes, and fightings about the law; for they are unprofitable and vain.
03:10 A factious man after a first and second admonition refuse;
03:11 knowing that such a one is perverted, and sinneth, being self-condemned.
03:12 When I shall send Artemas unto thee, or Tychicus, give diligence to come unto me to Nicopolis: for there I have determined to winter.
03:13 Set forward Zenas the lawyer and Apollos on their journey diligently, that nothing be wanting unto them.
03:14 And let our [people] also learn to maintain good works for necessary uses, that they be not unfruitful.
03:15 All that are with me salute thee. Salute them that love us in faith. Grace be with you all. Amen.
